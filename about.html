The Vision

For over 20 years I worked at the heart of global financial markets, brokering deals between the world's largest institutions. Global banks, hedge funds, energy companies, and trading houses. In commodities and most recently in oil, I operated in environments where speed, precision, and commercial clarity weren't optional. They were survival.

In that world, the biggest players had one significant advantage over everyone else. Access to the best technology, the best data, and the best tools. AI is simply the latest version of that advantage. But this time, it doesn't have to be that way.

I founded Reece AI because I watched small and medium-sized businesses get left behind. Not because they lacked ambition or talent, but because AI felt inaccessible, expensive, and wrapped in jargon that nobody had time to unpick.

That changes here. Reece AI exists to give every SME the same competitive edge that the world's biggest institutions already have. Practical, affordable AI that actually works for your business.
