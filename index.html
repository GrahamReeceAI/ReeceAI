FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need to know anything about AI to work with you?add
No. Most of our clients have tried ChatGPT once and stopped. That's the point of us. We work out where AI will help your business, build it, and show your team how to use it.
What size of business do you work with?add
UK small and medium businesses, typically 1 to 50 employees. Owner-operators, trades, contractors, service businesses, professional services. Anyone running a business they're personally invested in.
How long does it take to see results?add
Most clients see real time savings within 4 to 6 weeks of implementation. The first wins are usually in admin, quoting, customer responses, or content. We sequence the work so you see results fast and build from there.
Will AI replace my team?add
No. AI is best at the repetitive tasks that drain your team's time. The point is to free them up for higher-value work, not to remove them. Most of our clients use AI to grow without hiring, not to cut people.
Is my business data safe?add
Yes. We use enterprise-grade tools with proper data handling, and we never share your business data with anyone. Data privacy is built into how we design every solution.
What if I've tried AI tools before and they didn't stick?add
That's the most common starting point we see. The reason tools don't stick isn't usually the tool. It's that they were set up wrong, in the wrong place, or never properly built into how your team works. That's what we fix.
