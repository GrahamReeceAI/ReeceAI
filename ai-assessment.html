The 2-Hour
AI Assessment.

A structured session to map your business, find where AI would make the biggest difference, and tell you exactly what to do. In plain English, with a written report to take away.

What happens in the session

1

We map how your business currently runs

We go through your day-to-day: where your time goes, what your team does, how work moves through the business. No preparation needed from you.

2

We identify the highest-value AI opportunities

Not a list of every AI tool that exists. Specifically the two or three places in your business where AI would save the most time and have the clearest impact.

3

We agree on the right order to tackle them

Quick wins first. We sequence the opportunities so you see results fast, and build from there.

  • description

  • lightbulb

  • check_circle

Common Questions

What do I need to prepare for the AI Assessment?add
Nothing. Just show up. The session is built so you don't need any preparation. We go through how your business currently runs and identify the AI opportunities together.
How long does the AI Assessment take?add
Two hours over a single video call. The written findings report is sent within a few days after the session.
What's included in the written findings report?add
A detailed breakdown of every AI opportunity we've identified in your business. For each one we tell you what tools we'd use, what it would cost to implement, and the realistic timeframe to build it. It's a prioritised written plan you can act on, share with your team, or hand to anyone for implementation.
Can my team join the session?add
Yes, if it helps. Most clients prefer to do it one-to-one with the business owner, but if a senior team member would add value, bring them in.
What happens after the AI Assessment?add
Implementation is completely optional. You leave with a clear plan and you decide whether to take it further with us, do it in-house, or simply use the insights to inform your own next steps.
Why does the price vary between £299 and £999?add
The price is based on the size of your team. Larger businesses have more processes to map and more complex opportunities. The price is fixed and confirmed on your free call before you pay. No surprises.
